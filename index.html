A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Beyond the work

The person behind the projects.

JV_002PORTRAIT

I split my time between Benin City and Lagos, Nigeria, with a Bachelor's in Economics from the University of Ilorin behind me and a Postgraduate Diploma in Business Administration currently in progress. That mix, plus a genuine love for the blend of admin, design, and video work in front of me, is basically how I ended up here. There's something satisfying about turning a messy inbox or a rough clip into something clean and finished.

Outside of client work, my social life mostly runs through football and gaming, small circles, low noise. I'd call myself more introvert than extrovert, a lifestyle choice as much as anything else, and it shows in how I like to work too. I like systems that work quietly in the background, good scheduling, a tidy tracker, a well-timed cut, the kind of things people only notice when they're missing.

I work remote, across time zones, and I'm generally reachable and responsive.
